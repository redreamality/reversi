Copyright and DMCA Policy

We respect the intellectual property of others. If you believe something on the Reversi website infringes your copyright, open an issue on GitHub Issues with the title "Copyright notice". Issues are public, so please do not include private details such as your email address, home address or phone number.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so you can also send a formal notice straight to GitHub under its DMCA Takedown Policy. That route keeps your contact details out of public view.

What to include

  1. Your name and how you can be reached (replying in the issue is fine).
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed.
  3. The exact URL on this site where the material appears.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are authorised to act for the owner.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

What happens next

We review every complete notice and, where appropriate, remove or disable the material and let you know. If your material was removed and you believe this was a mistake, you can send a counter-notice the same way, with the details above.
