About Reversi

This website is a simple, quiet place to play Reversi in the browser, either against the computer or with a friend on the same screen. There are no accounts and nothing to install. Pages load the game only when you press Play.

Game engine

Game engine: UCThello by Oliver Merkel, MIT License. The rules engine and the AI, which uses Monte Carlo tree search (UCT), come from the open-source project UCThello. We did not write the engine; we built the board, the page design and the text around it. The board graphics, cover, icons and screenshots on this site were made for this site and are not taken from the UCThello project.
