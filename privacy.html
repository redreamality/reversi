Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Reversi website at https://redreamality.com/reversi/ ("the site", "we") collects when you visit and play.

What stays on your device

The game runs entirely in your browser. Moves, scores and the AI's calculations are not sent to us. The site stores one small item in your browser's local storage: whether you pressed Like. The site has no comment section at the moment and does not collect comments, messages or accounts. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and handles that data under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs. Requests to the site also pass through Cloudflare, which provides the domain's network and security service under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Visit statistics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count page views and three game events: play_start (you pressed Play), played_60s (you were still playing 60 seconds later) and fullscreen (you opened fullscreen), so we can see which pages are useful and whether the game loads well. These events do not include your moves, scores or anything you type. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, browser details and the pages you view; we only look at the totals. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

The site may show advertising to cover its costs. If ads are served by Google, Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and learn more on how Google uses data for ads.

Children

The site is a general-audience board game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so please do not include private details such as your email address, home address or phone number.
