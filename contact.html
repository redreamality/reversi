Contact

Found a bug, have a question about the rules, or want to suggest an improvement? Open an issue on GitHub Issues for this site. You need a free GitHub account to post. Issues are public, so please do not include private details such as your email address, home address or phone number.

Reporting a problem

It helps a lot if you include:

  • your device and browser (for example, "iPhone, Safari" or "Windows laptop, Chrome");
  • what you did and what happened instead;
  • a screenshot, if you can.

Other requests

For copyright complaints, please follow the steps on the Copyright / DMCA page. For privacy questions, see the Privacy Policy.
